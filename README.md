# FadeEight

Eight-bar live fade desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

The last chorus ends on a brick wall. Suno, Udio, and loop DAWs stop the file. People glue a second song on, or the record dies mid-breath. Session players stay eight more bars: the hook repeats, chairs leave one at a time, and the fader walks down.

Live samples only — FluidR3 acoustic grand, upright bass, nylon guitar, trumpet, violin, and an acoustic kit from the PreEight sample bank. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

Yesterday's ship was VerseEight (eight bars of verse in front of a locked hook) along with FromHook and DoorTwo. SeamFour, earlier the same morning, is the join inside a loop. FadeEight is the ending: not a verse, not a seam, not a loud ride-out.

Distinct from RideEight (energy stays up), ButtonFour (hard button), TagFour (last-line tag), EndEight (composed last eight), Exhale (organ/flute coda), DieTwice (false ending), HalfEight (half-time drop), and ModEight (last-chorus key change).

## Why this, from the room

- Songs keep ending suddenly — the chorus needed one more pass and a fade, not a cutoff.
- The other bad ending is two songs glued into one. A fade lets the record finish as itself.

## Play

1. Pick a locked chorus: Amber Last, Porch Last, or Fold Last.
2. Pick how the chairs leave.
3. Hear the hard cut, then the fade.
4. Copy the punch list or download MIDI and a 24-ish second WAV of chorus + fade.

Space plays the fade. Esc stops.
